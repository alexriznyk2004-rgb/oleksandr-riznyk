Attic apartment

A whole apartment under a pitched roof, designed at MBA Development Group: kitchen and dining, living room with a fireplace, bedroom, study and bathroom in one calm, warm palette.

My part
Interior concept and visualization: zoning, materials and finishes, furniture and lighting selection, rendering. Working drawings were outside my part.
Year
2024–2025

One concept for the whole apartment

Each room has its own mood, but they share one palette, one floor and one detail language, so the apartment reads as a whole.

Style
Modern classic: wall mouldings and panels combined with minimal kitchen fronts and warm wood.
Materials
Herringbone oak floor, light stone island, black-framed glass partitions, backlit dark wood panels, stone around the fireplace, green walls with wood in the study, beige plaster and walnut vanity in the bathroom.
Lighting
Daylight from roof windows, hidden LED lines in niches and panels, sculptural pendants over the dining table.
Software
3ds Max: modeling and scene. Corona Renderer: materials, light, render. Photoshop: post-production.
