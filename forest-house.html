Cottage

An exterior visualization of a minimalist house clad in dark timber, set in a meadow by a natural pond.

My part
Concept and visualization: modeling, materials, landscape, lighting, rendering, cinematic camera motion.
Year
2024–2025
ClayFinal

From clay to final

The clay render checks the massing, the timber fins and the light. The final keeps the same camera, so the mood comes from materials, mist and the reflection in the pond.

Drag the handle or use the arrow keys to compare.

Style
Minimalist modern house with vertical timber fins.
Materials
Dark timber cladding, black glazing, timber deck, natural pond, meadow grass.
Lighting
Low morning sun, mist in the trees, soft sky light.
Software
3ds Max: modeling and scene. Corona Renderer: materials, light, render. Photoshop: post-production.

Camera animation

A slow cinematic camera move built from the still render.
