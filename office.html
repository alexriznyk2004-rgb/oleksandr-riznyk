Office

An office interior designed at MBA Development Group: a glass meeting room wrapped in a slatted timber partition, and an open workspace with a moss wall.

My part
Interior concept and visualization: zoning, materials, furniture, rendering. Working drawings were outside my part.
Year
2024–2025

A meeting room inside the partition

A frameless glass meeting room on a timber podium by the window, framed by a stepped slatted canopy. The office stays open and full of daylight, and still gets a quiet zone.

Style
Warm minimalism with biophilic accents.
Zones
Workstations along the windows and walls, a meeting room for 8, a kitchen corner, a WC.
Materials
Timber slats, frameless glass, light oak floor, moss wall, white desks.
Software
3ds Max: modeling and scene. Corona Renderer: materials, light, render. Photoshop: post-production.
