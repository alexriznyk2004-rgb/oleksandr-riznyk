FRANKOF showroom

A furniture showroom for the FRANKOF factory, from the plan in 3ds Max to the built space. The hall is split into zones for bedroom, sofa, dining and lounge collections, with bookcase partitions carrying the brand.

My part
Showroom planning: zoning for collections, furniture layout and planograms, partitions and display elements, documentation for installation. Photos of the built showroom: FRANKOF.
Year
2025–2026

Zoning for collections

Each collection gets its own island on a rug, and the black bookcase partitions split the open hall without blocking the view, so a visitor sees the whole range from the entrance.

Showroom opening: FRANKOF on Instagram↗

Zones
Bedrooms, sofas and corner sofas, dining, lounge with armchairs, kids' corner.
Materials
Black-stained ash bookcases with a backlit logo, timber-clad columns, dark curtains, graphite carpet, green accent wall.
Lighting
Black track spotlights on an open ceiling, floor lamps and table lamps inside the zones.
Software
3ds Max: layout, planograms, presentation views. Archicad: drawings for wall colours, lighting and feature walls.
